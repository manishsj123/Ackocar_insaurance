Why do you need car insurance?

Car insurance is crucial to protect your vehicle and you from potential financial implications due to accident-related damages or third-party liabilities. Here are some of the reasons why you need insurance for cars.




Adherence to the law

The Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 mandates all motor vehicles, including electric cars, to be insured with Third-party Insurance. Failure to adhere to the law invites a penalty of up to Rs. 4,000 and/or imprisonment. Thus, it is essential that you buy/renew car insurance to comply with the law.

Shield against third-party liabilities

Third-party liabilities arise when your insured car causes damages to a third party’s property or injuries to a third party. Staying financially protected by insuring your car with 3rd Party Insurance (or Comprehensive Insurance) protects against financial burdens caused due to third-party liabilities.

Coverage against natural calamities

Floods, cyclones, earthquakes and other natural calamities can cause severe damage to your car, and the extent of the repair cost can be enormous. Comprehensive Car Insurance offers financial support for car repairs due to unexpected natural disasters.
